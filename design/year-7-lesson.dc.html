Input and conversion

Today we teach the computer to listen, and to turn words into numbers.

About 15 minutes
Hi, I'm Yoka! Let's get the computer listening.

For grown-ups: you don't need to know Python. Read the big idea aloud, then ask "what do you think will happen?" before every Run.

The big idea

input() is like a waiter taking your order. The program stops and waits until you answer. Whatever you type comes back as text, even if it looks like a number.
