## The big idea

**Indentation is the structure.** 4 spaces in means "inside the loop". 8 spaces means "inside the if, which is inside the loop".

- An **if inside a for** makes a fresh decision every time round.
- A **for inside a for** works like a car's mileage counter: the inner loop spins all the way round for every single click of the outer loop.
