## The big idea

A **condition** is a yes or no question. Python answers it with `True` or `False`.

| Operator | Means | True when |
|---|---|---|
| `==` | is equal to | `5 == 5` |
| `!=` | is not equal to | `5 != 4` |
| `>` | greater than | `7 > 3` |
| `<` | less than | `3 < 7` |
| `>=` | greater than or equal to | `5 >= 5` |
| `<=` | less than or equal to | `4 <= 5` |

`if`, `elif` and `else` are a **road junction**. Python checks each sign in order and takes the **first** road whose answer is True. Every other road is skipped.

<Note title="= is not ==">
`mark = 70` **sets** the box. `mark == 70` **asks** a question.
</Note>
